# Cardora

### Your Global Gift Card Marketplace

Cardora is an online gift card marketplace where customers can buy gift cards directly from Cardora or purchase gift cards listed by independent sellers.

## Our Services

- Official gift card store
- Customer-to-customer marketplace
- Buyer and seller accounts
- Secure payment processing
- Seller dashboard
- Order management
- Gift card verification
- Customer support and dispute resolution

## Gift Card Categories

- Apple Gift Cards
- Amazon Gift Cards
- Google Play
- Steam
- Xbox
- PlayStation
- Netflix
- Razer Gold

## Our Mission

To make buying and selling gift cards simple, accessible and secure for everyone.
